# AI Review Record

This record was updated after reviewing the complete preprint, its LaTeX source, the compiled PDF, and the supporting repository artifacts. Three separate review passes were completed with **Codex**: practitioner, probability, and preprint. Each pass used the required checklist. Comments were checked against the available evidence before being accepted or rejected; acceptance means the comment should influence the next revision, not that every AI suggestion was accepted automatically.

4. Strengthen the evaluation with repeated paired runs, uncertainty intervals, natural-prevalence cases, and repository-level holdouts.
5. Address unknown and simultaneous causes, Code/Test ambiguity, uneven state coverage, and the 55% human-review workload.
6. Keep the system read-only and add stakeholder, privacy, security, monitoring, ethics, and escalation controls before any live pilot.
7. Improve Figure 1 readability and replace the placeholder PDF metadata before publication.
